## P5 What Is an FDE?

# An FDE starts with an unclear business problem and stays until the result can be tested

A Forward Deployed Engineer does more than write software at a customer site.

The work begins by observing the business: why leadership wants AI, what employees do each day, where the data sits, and where time, errors, or slow customer response are hurting the company.

Once the problem is clear, the FDE chooses the simplest suitable solution:

- Use an existing product when it already solves the problem.
- Build a Skill when a Skill is enough.
- Integrate with existing systems when the workflow crosses tools.
- Create a database or knowledge base when the data needs to be organized first.
- Design a human-agent workflow when an agent can take on part of the work.

- Operational databases
- Enterprise knowledge bases
- Data cleanup and consistent naming

Best for: Companies with plenty of information that is hard to find, share, or trust.

### A company-wide AI program

- Identify work that is suitable for AI across departments
- Redesign how people and agents divide the work
- Set permissions, approvals, and accountability records
- Define the pilot sequence and expansion plan
- Train the company’s own Agent Bosses

Best for: Leadership teams that want a coordinated program instead of isolated experiments.

## P7 How We Start

# Assess first. Build only when the opportunity is clear.

### 1.

We clarify what the company wants to change: time, errors, revenue, customer response, or the way the organization works.

### 2.

We look at the spreadsheets, documents, messages, and business systems employees use every day, including the exceptions that never appear in a process manual.

### 3.

We compare opportunities by business value, delivery difficulty, data readiness, risk, and time to validation. Not every problem needs AI.

### 4.

One workflow, one accountable owner, real data, a defined scope, and a clear way to judge the result.

### 5.

After real users have tested the pilot, the company can improve it, expand it, or stop.

## P8 What a Pilot Includes

# More than a demo: a working test with clear evidence

Depending on the agreed scope, a pilot may include:

- A map of the current workflow
- The problem and the exact boundary of the pilot
- A working prototype or system
- The data and permissions used
- Clear responsibilities for people and agents
- Human approval points
- A method for comparing before and after
- A recommendation to continue, adjust, or stop

Before work begins, we agree on:

- Who owns the outcome
- What completion means
- What is outside the pilot
- How the result will be judged

## P10 Follow-On Service: Agent Boss Training

# A working agent still needs someone who knows how to lead it

FDE delivery builds and launches the AI transformation.

After launch, the company’s managers and employees need to know how to:

- Give agents clear goals and completion criteria
- Decide which work can be delegated
- Review the process and the result
- Handle exceptions, request changes, or stop a task
- Manage data, tools, and permissions
- Turn useful practices into shared Skills
- Remain accountable for the business outcome

We call this training **Agent Boss**.

It is not a prompt-writing class or a tour of AI tools. It teaches people how to lead work carried out by both humans and agents.

```text
FDE: Build and launch the new way of working
Agent Boss: Help the team take ownership and keep improving it
```
