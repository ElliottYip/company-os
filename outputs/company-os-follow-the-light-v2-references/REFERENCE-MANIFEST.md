# Company OS — Follow the Light V2 reference manifest

Generation mode: built-in image generation tool.

## Style contract

- Preset: realistic documentary cinema + restrained premium technology advertisement
- Frame intent: horizontal 2.39:1 composition
- Palette progression: cold blue predawn → neutral daylight → restrained amber sunset
- Camera texture: large-format location photography with subtle natural 35mm texture
- Human scale: tiny and incidental in wide landscapes; ordinary, candid and anatomically natural
- Light rule: only physically believable sunlight and reflection; never a visible data beam
- Forbidden: sci-fi UI, holograms, tourism-poster saturation, HDR halos, plastic people, luxury digital-nomad clichés
