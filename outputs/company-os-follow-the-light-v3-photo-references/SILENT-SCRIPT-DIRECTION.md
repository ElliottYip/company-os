# Company OS — Follow the Light

## Silent-film direction V2

No voice-over. No dialogue. No background music. Natural ambience and restrained device sounds only.

## Core proposition

People focus on ideas, direction, taste, and judgment. Agents absorb complex execution and communication overhead. Company OS preserves context, coordinates the work, and makes end-to-end remote software building possible.

The film must not read as “working while travelling.” It should show that a person can leave the workstation because the work continues without their constant presence.

## Visual argument

1. **An idea begins with a person.** At the real Lake Louise location, a person sketches a product idea in a notebook. Pencil, paper, and judgment are foregrounded; coding is not.
2. **Direction is given once.** From a side view of a black-screen Apple MacBook Pro, the person makes one deliberate trackpad action. Real Company OS UI will be composited later.
3. **Agents carry the complexity.** The person is already travelling through the real Jungfrau snow landscape while software implementation, testing, documentation, and coordination progress without meetings.
4. **Context crosses distance.** The work reaches another person at the real Faroe Islands location with all prior context intact; no handoff call, chat thread, or status meeting is required.
5. **People return only for judgment.** A side-view Apple iPhone receives one decision request. The person makes one choice and returns the phone to a pocket.
6. **Execution resolves itself.** Against the real Grand Canyon landscape, the system finishes the build, tests, documentation, and release preparation.
7. **The result arrives, not the noise.** On the real surfing coast, people continue living while the completed software work becomes ready to ship.

## Minimal post-composited UI copy

Only these short product-state phrases may appear inside the black device screens during post-production:

- `Build the new onboarding flow.`
- `Delegate`
- `Implementing`
- `Testing`
- `Documenting`
- `Context synchronized`
- `Decision needed`
- `Use passkeys by default?`
- `Approve`
- `Build passed`
- `Ready to ship`

No email, Slack-like chat, video calls, typing bubbles, unread badges, long dashboards, or decorative sci-fi animation.

## Sound narrative

Pencil on paper → trackpad click → laptop hinge → train rail rhythm → Atlantic wind → one phone vibration → one touch → boot lace pull → ocean surf → silence.

The absence of notification noise and conversation is part of the product message: communication overhead has disappeared.
